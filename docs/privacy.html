Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 15, 2026

This policy covers Aviator Digital (com.jglenn9k.aviator.watchface) and Aviator Sensors (com.jglenn9k.aviator.sensors), published by James Glenn under the Google Play developer name thedonkdonk.

Data access and use

Aviator Sensors reads the watch's rotation-vector sensor to calculate a magnetic heading and its pressure sensor to calculate raw ambient pressure. These readings are used only to display the compass, pressure dashboard, and Wear OS complications on the user's watch.

Aviator Digital can display complication data chosen by the user. Blood oxygen and weather data are supplied directly by independently installed providers such as Samsung Health and Samsung Weather. These apps and their privacy practices are controlled by their respective publishers.

Collection and sharing

The apps do not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information, health information, location, sensor readings, identifiers, diagnostics, or usage data. They contain no advertising, analytics, account system, or developer-operated network service.

Storage, security, retention, and deletion

The latest heading and pressure readings and their timestamps are cached in Android app-private storage on the watch. Android's application sandbox protects this storage. The data is not transmitted off the device. A newer reading replaces the previous reading. Users can delete all cached data at any time by clearing Aviator Sensors storage or uninstalling the app. The apps do not create user accounts and retain no server-side data.

Children

The apps are general-purpose utilities and are not directed to children. They do not knowingly collect information from children or any other users.

Changes

Material changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact

Privacy questions may be submitted through the project's public issue tracker.

These apps are not certified flight instruments. Do not use them for flight safety or medical decisions.
